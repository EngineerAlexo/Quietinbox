PRIVACY

What Quiet Inbox does and does not do

Clear limits, so you know exactly what to expect before you install.

1

It only captures notifications

Quiet Inbox sees only the text that the messaging apps place in notifications. It cannot read chats directly.

2

It never sends messages

No automatic replies and no messages sent on your behalf. Replies happen only when you open the original app yourself.

3

Stored only on your device

Captured previews are saved locally. They are not uploaded to a Quiet Inbox server.

4

Read receipts are not guaranteed

Quiet Inbox cannot guarantee that senders won't see that a notification was shown or that a message was read.

HELP CENTER

Frequently asked questions

Useful information before downloading the app.

What is Quiet Inbox?

Quiet Inbox is a privacy-focused Android app. It saves incoming message previews from imo, Telegram, WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger into one separate inbox, so you can read them without opening each app.

Does it open my messages or chats?

No. Quiet Inbox only captures what appears in notifications. It does not open the original messaging apps or read your full chat history.

Why does it need Notification Access?

Android requires Notification Access before an app can read notifications from other apps. You can turn this permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

What gets captured?

Only the text the messaging app shows in its notification, such as the sender and the message preview. Some apps show only a generic notice like "New message", or hide content when the phone is locked. In those cases, Quiet Inbox can only save what is shown.

Will the sender know I read their message?

Quiet Inbox cannot guarantee that. Read receipts depend on the messaging app and its settings. Reading a preview in Quiet Inbox may or may not count as reading the message, depending on the app.

Does Quiet Inbox send replies for me?

No. It never sends messages or replies automatically. Marking a message to reply later only saves a reminder in the app.

Where are my saved previews stored?

Captured previews are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to a Quiet Inbox server. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Why does Android show an installation warning?

Android warns you about apps installed outside Google Play. Make sure you downloaded the APK from this official page before proceeding, and never ignore a security warning without understanding it.

Do I need internet to use Quiet Inbox?

Quiet Inbox works on your device and does not need internet to show saved previews. The messaging apps still need internet to deliver the notifications it captures.
